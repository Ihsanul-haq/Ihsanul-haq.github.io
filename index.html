About

I hold an MSc in Energy Engineering (Distinction) from the University of Hull and a BSc in Electrical Engineering from the University of Engineering and Technology, Peshawar. My research applies deep learning to power and energy systems, including load forecasting, fault diagnosis and renewable energy management, alongside computer vision for healthcare and safety applications. I continue to collaborate remotely with a research group at UET Peshawar on several of these projects.

Projects

Grouped by research direction. Full repositories are linked from my GitHub.

Energy systems, smart grids & power systems

Short-term load forecasting with deep learning
Compared LSTM, GRU, LSTM-CNN and GRU-CNN architectures for short-term electricity load forecasting. Evaluated forecasting accuracy to support smart-grid demand planning. Formed my MSc dissertation and was published at IEEE CASE 2023.
ML-based renewable forecasting and priority load management
Combined renewable-energy forecasting with priority-based load management in a single ML pipeline. Aimed at improving reliability of smart energy systems under variable generation. Published in the International Journal of Innovations in Science & Technology.
Unified framework for load forecasting and fault diagnosis
Built a combined framework integrating real-time load forecasting with multi-class fault diagnosis for smart grids. Designed to support simultaneous demand monitoring and predictive maintenance. A collaborative contribution within a wider research group.
Sustainable energy management in smart grids
Investigated machine-learning strategies for sustainable, efficient energy management under renewable integration. Explored optimisation of energy usage across a simulated smart-grid setting. Published in Spectrum of Engineering Sciences.
ML-based fault detection in transmission lines and electric machines
Applied machine-learning classifiers to detect faults in three-phase transmission lines and electric machines. Aimed at earlier fault identification to improve power-system reliability. Shared as an open-access record on Zenodo.

Computer vision & deep learning for healthcare

AI-powered smart glasses for the visually impaired
Contributed to a computer-vision system built into smart glasses to assist visually impaired users. Combined real-time scene understanding with audio feedback for navigation and object recognition. Published in the International Journal of Innovations in Science & Technology.
Virtual keyboard and mouse for partially disabled patients
Co-developed a vision-based virtual keyboard and mouse for patients with limited hand mobility. Used deep-learning-based tracking to translate gesture or gaze input into device control. Published as part of an assistive-technology research line.
Chest X-ray classification
Personal project applying convolutional neural networks to classify chest X-ray images for disease detection. Trained and evaluated models on medical-imaging data for diagnostic support. Used to explore CNN architectures for healthcare imaging tasks.

Applied AI for real-world systems

Computer-vision child safety solution using YOLOv8
Developed a YOLOv8-based detection system for real-time child-safety monitoring. Focused on reliable person and object detection under practical, real-world conditions. Published in 2025.
TITANIUM-4: low-latency intrusion detection pipeline
Co-authored a four-layer, low-latency network intrusion-detection pipeline. Examined how benchmark-trained detection thresholds hold up under domain shift in deployment. Published in 2026.

Wireless communication & antenna design

High-gain microstrip patch antenna design
Designed and simulated a microstrip patch antenna in CST Studio Suite as my BSc final-year project. Optimised substrate and geometry to improve gain and return loss. Provided a foundation in RF design and electromagnetic simulation.
